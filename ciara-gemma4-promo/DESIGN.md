# CIARA Gemma 4 Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark, technical, high-contrast product promo for a local-first AI desktop agent. The visual language comes from the CIARA landing page: black canvas, neon lime signal color, faint grid/scanline overlays, glass panels, terminal-like status text, and bold compressed uppercase headlines. The video should feel like a working product demo for a serious hackathon submission: cinematic, fast, credible, and impact-driven.

## Colors

- Background: `#020302`
- Deep panel: `rgba(5, 12, 9, 0.86)`
- Strong panel: `#0b130d`
- Gemma/CIARA lime: `#a6ff00`
- Secondary lime: `#78d600`
- Primary text: `#f6f7f1`
- Muted text: `#b3b8ad`
- Hairline: `rgba(166, 255, 0, 0.34)`

## Typography

- Display: `Impact`, `"Arial Black"`, `Haettenschweiler`, sans-serif
- Body/UI: `Inter`, `system-ui`, `"Segoe UI"`, sans-serif
- Code/status: `"Cascadia Code"`, `"Consolas"`, monospace

## Motion

- Snap-to-grid technical motion.
- Strong entrance animations with y-offset, scale, and opacity.
- Lime scan wipes between scenes.
- Panels should feel like real product surfaces, not generic slides.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic blue/purple AI gradients.
- Do not make it feel like a landing-page carousel.
- Do not use vague AI phrases without showing workflow structure.
- Do not hide Gemma 4 in small text. Gemma 4 must be central.
- Do not use friendly pastel SaaS styling.
